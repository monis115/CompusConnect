Governance Principles

Centralized Control

CampusConnect enforces a centralized control model for compliance-critical functions while maintaining a college-centric workflow for academic and admission operations.

Clear Accountability

Single active users for Document Verification and Accountant roles ensure clear accountability and consistent decision-making across all operations.

Audit Consistency

Controlled operational governance ensures complete audit trails and maintains compliance with regulatory requirements and institutional policies.

Scalable Operations

Multiple Visa and Telex Officers allow workload distribution while maintaining centralized oversight and operational efficiency at scale.

College Coordinator Assignments

University Coordinators are assigned to colleges to manage student applications and admission letters. Each college must have an active coordinator to receive forwarded students.
